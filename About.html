About Us

Who we are:

Ubuntu Outdoor Adventures was founded in 2010 with a passion for sharing South Africa's natural beauty. Based in Johannesburg, we operate guided outdoor experiences across South Africa, from the Drakensberg mountains to the Cape winelands.

Our mission:

To connect people with nature through safe, professionally guided outdoor adventures that create lasting memories and inspire a love for the outdoors.

Why choose us:

  • Expert certified guides with years of experience
  • Small groups for a personal experience
  • Safety is our top priority on every trip
  • Suitable for all fitness levels and ages
  • Proudly South African, supporting local communities
